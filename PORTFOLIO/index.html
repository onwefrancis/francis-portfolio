02 · About

Engineer's rigour, writer's clarity, builder's hands.

Petroleum Engineering graduate (B.Eng., Second Class Upper, FUTO) with hands-on experience across upstream production operations, drilling-fluids laboratory research and regulatory compliance — combined with 3+ years of freelance technical writing that turns complex engineering and scientific subject matter into clear, accurate, audience-friendly English.

By day I keep 50+ products compliant at NCI Pharm Chem (NAFDAC registrations, renewals, CTD dossiers). Before that I reviewed operator production reports and field-development plans at NUPRC, ran 15+ drilling-fluid tests in FUTO's petroleum lab, and led NUESA as Chapters Executive President.

My rule for every tool I ship: it may only claim what it can prove. Dashboards cite their source, AI answers label themselves guidance-only, and regulated software stays honest about its validation status.
